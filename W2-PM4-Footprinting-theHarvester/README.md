**W2-PM4 – Footprinting with theHarvester**

**Objective**

To understand how theHarvester supports reconnaissance by collecting publicly available information about a target domain.

**Tool Used**
- theHarvester

**Practical Overview**

This practical focused on using theHarvester to gather publicly available information during the footprinting and reconnaissance stage of ethical hacking.

**Practical Evidence**

Screenshots of the completed practical activities and relevant tool outputs will be uploaded to this folder.

**Key Learning Outcomes**
- Understanding Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT).
- Learning how theHarvester supports information gathering.
- Understanding domain-related reconnaissance.
- Recognising the importance of publicly exposed information in cybersecurity.

**Conclusion**

This practical improved my understanding of information gathering and demonstrated how theHarvester can support reconnaissance activities during a cybersecurity assessment.
